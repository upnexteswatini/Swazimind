No downloads, no setup calls. Your account, your apps, your rules.

Your consent, in plain language

What giving us the code actually means.

Before a connection is created we show you the full list. When you paste the verification code back, you are agreeing to it. Nothing hidden, nothing pre-ticked.

By entering and submitting the verification code, you allow SwaziMind to manage your connected account and to:

  • Read messages and emails in the connected account
  • Search your mailbox, files and calendar
  • Draft replies, documents and summaries in your name
  • Create and edit calendar events and reminders
  • Send, forward or reply to messages only after you approve each one
  • Archive, label and organise items you approve
  • Store the access token needed to keep the connection alive

You can revoke any of this at any time from Connections. Revoking a code-based connection invalidates the token immediately.
